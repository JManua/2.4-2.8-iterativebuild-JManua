Our purpose

Our aim is for Tawa College to be a school where all students have access to meaningful learning experiences that help shape and form their ability to critically engage with, and understand, their world. Tawa College is a school that ultimately deals with people and their potential. Through EOTC, we hope to provide students with new learning opportunities that are fun while allowing them to grow and teaching them new skills. We believe we should be advocates for students as well as agents of change and development.

What is EOTC?

As part of the Year 10 programme students will be participating in four days of Education Outside the Classroom (EOTC). The focus of this programme is one of citizenship and social awareness. The four days of activities in which your son/daughter will participate are as follows: Community Service, Rangituhi Walk, Adrenaline Forest/Te Rauparaha Arena Aquatic Centre and Wainuiomata Camp.
